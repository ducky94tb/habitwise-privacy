Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Mosaica has no sign-in of any kind. Everything you do stays on your device.
  • Does it need an internet connection? No. The whole game, including puzzles made from your own photos, works fully offline.
  • Will my progress transfer to a new phone? No. Because data is kept only on your device and never synced to a server, your progress, coins, and saved puzzles do not move to a new device. This is a deliberate trade-off so that we never have to collect your data.
  • What happens to photos I use for a puzzle? Mosaica copies the single photo you pick into its own private storage, scales it down, and never sends it anywhere. Deleting that photo inside the app removes the copy and any puzzles made from it. See the Privacy Policy for details.
  • How do I reset my progress or delete my data? See the delete data page.
  • Are there ads or in-app purchases? Not in the current build. If that ever changes, the store listing and the Privacy Policy will say so before it ships.
  • Where do the puzzle photos come from? From the catalog bundled with the app. Photographer credit and licence for each one is listed in-app under Settings → About → Credits.
