Delete your data

Mosaica

Mosaica has no user accounts and no server that stores your data — no sign-up, no sign-in, no sync. There is therefore no "account" to delete remotely. Everything lives on your own device, and you can remove it yourself at any time.

1. Reset progress, but keep the app

This removes your coins, statistics, achievements, streaks, and any saved puzzles in progress, while leaving the app installed:

  1. Open Mosaica.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Reset all progress and confirm.

2. Delete a photo puzzle

To remove a specific photo you turned into a puzzle, delete it from Your Photos in the app. This removes the private copy of that photo and any puzzles saved from it.

3. Delete everything

Choose either method:

  • Uninstall the app. This wipes all app data on that device.
  • Clear the app's data from your OS while keeping the app installed: go to System Settings → Apps → Mosaica → Storage → Clear data. The exact path varies slightly by manufacturer and OS version.

Both methods are irreversible.
