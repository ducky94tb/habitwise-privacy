Privacy Policy

Mosaica · Last updated October 26, 2026

Mosaica is a jigsaw puzzle game. It has no account and no sign-in, and your puzzles, progress and photos stay on your device. The game shows ads, served by Google AdMob, to stay free; AdMob processes some device data to do that, described below. Every puzzle still plays offline — without a connection there are simply no ads.

Advertising

Mosaica shows two kinds of ad:

  • a rewarded ad you can choose to watch for a hint when you are out of coins, and
  • an occasional full-screen ad between puzzles — never during one, and never in your first puzzles.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure them, Google may process your device’s advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID; on iOS, the IDFA only if you allow tracking), your IP address and the approximate location it implies, information about your device and the app, and how you interact with the ads.

Whether ads are personalised depends on your choices:

  • In the EU, the UK and other regions where it is required, Mosaica asks for consent on first launch with Google’s consent form. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • On iOS, Apple’s prompt asks whether Mosaica may track you. You can change that in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you do not allow it, ads are not personalised with the IDFA.

Ad content is limited to Google’s “PG” rating. Mosaica itself receives none of this data, and nothing about your puzzles or photos is shared with Google or anyone else.

What is stored on your device

Everything the game remembers is written to the app’s own private storage on your device:

WhatWhere
Settings (theme, sound, accessibility)app-private storage
Coins, statistics, achievements, streaksapp-private storage
Saved puzzles in progressapp-private storage
Photos you turn into puzzlesapp-private storage, as a copy
How many ads you have seen, for the limits aboveapp-private storage

None of it is uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. Settings → Reset all progress deletes it without uninstalling.

Photos

When you choose a photo to make a puzzle from, Mosaica copies it into its own private folder, scales it down for play, and makes a thumbnail. The copy never leaves your device and is not readable by other apps. Deleting the photo inside Mosaica deletes the copy and any saved puzzles made from it.

Mosaica never browses your photo library. The system photo picker shows you your own library and hands the app back only the single image you select.

Age

Mosaica is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Analytics and purchases

Mosaica has no analytics of its own and no in-app purchases in this version.
