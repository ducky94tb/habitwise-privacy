Privacy Policy

Mosaica · Last updated October 24, 2026

Mosaica is an offline jigsaw puzzle game. This page explains what the app collects — which is nothing — and what it stores on your own device.

What Mosaica does not collect

  • No account, no sign-in. There is nothing to register and nothing to sign into.
  • We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or any other personal information.
  • The app shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics SDK in this build.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties — there is no server to share it from.
  • The shipped Android app declares no internet permission, so it is technically unable to send anything off your device.

What is stored, and where

Everything the game remembers is written to the app's own private storage on your device:

WhatWhere
Settings (theme, sound, accessibility)app-private storage
Coins, statistics, achievements, streaksapp-private storage
Saved puzzles in progressapp-private storage
Photos you turn into puzzlesapp-private storage, as a copy

None of it is synced anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. Settings → Reset all progress deletes it without uninstalling.

Photos

When you choose a photo to make a puzzle from, Mosaica copies it into its own private folder, scales it down for play, and makes a thumbnail. The copy never leaves your device and is not readable by other apps. Deleting the photo inside Mosaica deletes the copy and any saved puzzles made from it.

Mosaica never browses your photo library. The system photo picker shows you your own library and hands the app back only the single image you select.

Children

Mosaica is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data, there is no age-gated data processing to describe.

If ads or analytics are added later

The code keeps analytics and advertising behind interfaces whose shipped implementations currently do nothing. If a future release wires in a real provider, that release will add the internet permission (visible in the store listing and the app's permissions), update this page before publishing, and offer an opt-out — with an App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS where required.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a revised "last updated" date.
